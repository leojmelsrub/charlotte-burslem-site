About

About Charlotte — your Portland bagpiper

Charlotte plays with the Portland Metro Youth Pipe Band — the youth program of the Portland Metro Pipe Band, Oregon's only Grade 2 competitive pipe band, which has competed at the World Pipe Band Championships in Glasgow and shared a stage with The Chieftains. That's the company she trains in.

She's a competitive Grade 4 piper, has been playing since 2019, and competes across the Pacific Northwest, training closely under Pipe Major Mark Tomasetti. It started early: she grew up tagging along to local Highland Games with her Scottish grandmother, and the pipes stuck — it turned into the thing she'd happily do every weekend.

Booking a young piper on her way up has its own kind of charm — you get someone who takes the music seriously and the day joyfully, in full Highland dress, on time, and genuinely glad to be there.

Questions, answered

How much does it cost to hire a bagpiper in Portland?

Hiring Charlotte starts at $350 for weddings, $300 for parties, graduations, retirements and corporate events, and $200 for funerals. Travel beyond the Portland metro and any extra time are quoted up front, and listed prices are starting points confirmed in a written quote before you book.

What events does a bagpiper play?

Charlotte plays weddings, parties, graduations, retirements, corporate and community events, and funerals and memorials. If it's a moment worth marking, bagpipes fit.

Can a bagpiper play at my wedding?

Yes — weddings are one of Charlotte's favorites. She pipes the guest gathering, the processional, the recessional, and leads everyone to the reception. Tell us the moments you want piped and we'll plan it together.

Does Charlotte play parties, graduations and retirements?

Yes. These are lively, celebratory sets, and she's happy to learn a special request with enough notice.

What areas does Charlotte serve?

Charlotte serves Portland and the surrounding metro, including Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham, Lake Oswego, Tigard, Milwaukie, Oregon City and Vancouver, WA. She travels a little farther for the right event — just ask.

What's included when you hire Charlotte?

Charlotte arrives early in full Highland dress and plays for around 30 minutes for celebrations. The exact tunes and timing are confirmed with you beforehand.

How do I book a bagpiper, and who do I talk to?

Send the booking form or call or text. Charlotte is 16, so her parent handles all booking and communication — you arrange everything with a responsible adult throughout. A 50% deposit reserves your date.

How far in advance should I book a bagpiper?

For weddings and parties, a few weeks to a few months gives the best chance at your date. Shorter notice is often possible — just ask.

Can a bagpiper play indoors or with other musicians?

Yes to both. Bagpipes are loud and carry beautifully outdoors, and work indoors in the right space — we'll advise on the venue, and Charlotte has played alongside other musicians.

What experience does Charlotte have?

Charlotte is a competitive Grade 4 bagpiper with the Portland Metro Youth Pipe Band — the youth program of Oregon's only Grade 2 competitive band. She's played since 2019 and competes across the Pacific Northwest.
